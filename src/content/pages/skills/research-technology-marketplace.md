🛒**Research Technology Marketplace**
The Research Technology Marketplace is your gateway to the latest research technologies, computational tools, and shared resources across the CUNY system. Our goal is to empower researchers at every level—students, postdocs, and faculty—to accelerate discovery and innovation by providing easy access to advanced equipment and expertise.

Whether you are launching a new project, scaling up your experiments, or seeking specialized instrumentation, the Marketplace connects you to a robust network of resources. We facilitate collaboration and resource sharing, helping you maximize the impact of your research while minimizing barriers to access.

* 🖥️ **Available Major Equipment:** Gain access to high-performance computing clusters for data-intensive research, next-generation sequencing machines for genomics, and a wide array of specialized research equipment to support your scientific goals.
* 🏢 **Accessible Core Facilities:** Take advantage of shared core facilities, including imaging centers, analytical labs, and fabrication spaces, all available to CUNY researchers through a streamlined booking process.
* 🔄 **Equipment Exchange:** Our platform enables the sharing and exchange of research equipment across CUNY institutions, reducing duplication and ensuring that valuable resources are fully utilized. List your available equipment or find what you need for your next project.
